## Course Description

Computer Vision Smart City is a unique hands-on course where you build a physical miniature city from printable A4 graphics and off-the-shelf toy cars, then deploy real computer vision to manage it intelligently. You will train custom AI models for top-down mini car detection, build a modular city layout, and program smart parking, traffic lights, barriers, and vehicle tracking systems. Arduino integration allows you to control physical traffic lights and gates directly from Python. Ideal for schools, universities, and serious hobbyists.
