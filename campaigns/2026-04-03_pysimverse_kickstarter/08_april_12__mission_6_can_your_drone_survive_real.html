What You'll Build in Mission 6

Navigate a real obstacle course — entirely in code:

  • Dynamic Obstacle Layout: Fly through a course with placed barriers, pillars, and narrow gaps — different each run to test your logic robustly.
  • Depth-Aware Navigation: Use distance sensing data from the simulator to detect obstacles ahead and plan around them in real time.
  • Reactive Path Adjustment: When an obstacle is detected, your drone reroutes automatically — no pre-mapped paths, just live decision-making.
  • Full-Stack Autonomy: This mission combines perception, control, and planning — giving you a taste of how real autonomous systems are architected.
