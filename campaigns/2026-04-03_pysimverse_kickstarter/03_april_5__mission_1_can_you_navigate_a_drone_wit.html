What You'll Learn in Mission 1

The skills you build here are the foundation everything else runs on:

  • Takeoff & Hover Logic: Before your drone does anything useful, it needs to get off the ground and stay there. This is where that starts.
  • Waypoint Navigation: Define a sequence of 3D coordinates — your drone follows. That same logic is what later missions build vision and autonomy on top of.
  • Collision-Free Pathing: Spatial reasoning isn't optional when five more missions get progressively harder. Getting this right now matters later.
  • Precision Landing: It sounds simple. It isn't. And when Mission 3 asks your drone to land on a moving target, you'll be glad you started here.
