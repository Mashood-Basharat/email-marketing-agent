You've seen them at the Super Bowl, Olympics, and major brand launches — hundreds of drones forming logos, animations, and patterns in the night sky.

That's not magic. That's programming.

In this mission, you'll learn to choreograph drone formations and animate light patterns — the same logic used by production-level show teams. You'll go from "that looks impossible" to "I built that."

🌾 MISSION: AGRICULTURE MAPPING

Farmers using drone tech are out-producing those who don't. They're mapping fields faster, identifying problem zones earlier, and covering more ground with less effort.

In this mission, you'll simulate crop area mapping and field coverage analysis — the core logic behind real agricultural drone systems. Simple to learn. Genuinely useful. Already deployed in the real world.

Smart cities are being built on drone-based monitoring — counting vehicles, measuring traffic density, identifying congestion patterns before they become gridlock.

In this mission, you'll build exactly that kind of AI monitoring system. The logic is straightforward. The application is massive. When you finish this mission, you'll understand how modern traffic intelligence actually works.
