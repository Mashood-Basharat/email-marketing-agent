What You'll Learn in Mission 2

Adding eyes to your drone — and why that changes everything that comes after:

  • Virtual Camera Mount: You attach a simulated camera to the drone and start streaming image frames from mid-air. Simple to set up — but it's what every later mission depends on.
  • Frame Capture & Processing: Pull live frames with OpenCV while the drone is flying. This is the same pipeline real aerial systems use — you're just not risking a $2,000 rig to learn it.
  • Visual Feedback Loop: The drone starts reacting to what it sees. That's not a feature — it's the shift from scripted movement to actual autonomy. Mission 3 takes that further.
  • Snapshot on Command: Trigger image saves at specific waypoints. It sounds like a small thing until you realise that's exactly how inspection drones and mapping systems work in production.
