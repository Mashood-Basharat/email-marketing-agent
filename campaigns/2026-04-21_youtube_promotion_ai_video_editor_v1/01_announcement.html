Editing gets slow the moment B‑roll starts.

Manual B‑roll searching is time‑consuming, repetitive, and mentally exhausting. You pause your flow, open stock sites, try keyword after keyword, download a few clips, then realise they don’t match the sentence — and you repeat that loop for the entire video.

This AI Video Editor automates the workflow end‑to‑end — transcription → B‑roll suggestions → smart timeline placement — while still letting you customise every decision.

Here’s how it works in a way you can actually visualise. You upload a video and the editor generates a full transcript first. That transcript becomes the “map” of your timeline — what’s being said, in what order, and what each moment is about.

Then the AI pulls out smart keywords from the transcript and suggests relevant B‑roll for each section — not random visuals, but footage that matches the context of the sentence. It also places those clips on the timeline intelligently: intro moments get visuals that fit the start, and emotional/frustration beats get emphasis where they actually happen.

Finally, you stay in control. You can change keywords, replace suggested footage, search your own custom clips, preview the full edit, and only then export — either as a finished video or as a timeline you can keep editing in other tools.
