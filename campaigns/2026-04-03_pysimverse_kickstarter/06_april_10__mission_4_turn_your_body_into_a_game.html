What You'll Build in Mission 4

Turn body pose into real drone commands:

  • Full-Body Pose Estimation: Detect 33 body keypoints in real time using MediaPipe Pose — shoulders, hips, knees, and more.
  • Tilt-to-Direction Mapping: Translate body lean angles into flight vectors — making movement feel natural and intuitive.
  • Speed Scaling: The more you lean, the faster the drone moves — a proportional control system built entirely in Python.
  • Stable Hover Logic: Stand neutral and the drone holds position automatically — your posture becomes a digital throttle hold.
