What You'll Build in Mission 5

Vision-based navigation with no manual control:

  • Downward Camera Feed: Point your simulated drone's camera at the ground and use OpenCV to isolate a colored line in real time.
  • Line Detection Algorithm: Find the center of the line using contour detection, then calculate the drone's offset from it.
  • Proportional Steering: Use the offset value to steer the drone back toward the center — an entry-level PID-style control system.
  • Fully Autonomous Loop: The drone detects, calculates, and corrects — all on its own, every frame, without any human input.
