The Trend You Cannot Ignore

Every major AI lab is moving intelligence off the cloud.

Apple runs language models entirely on-device. Google ships Gemini Nano on phones. Meta is embedding AI into glasses and wearables. The era of cloud-dependent AI is ending — and every engineer who knows how to deploy AI at the edge is suddenly in very high demand.

Why This Matters to You

Right now, most developers building with AI are doing it the same way: call an API, wait for a response, pay per token. It works — until it doesn't. APIs go down. Costs scale unpredictably. Data leaves your hardware. And in environments like hospitals, factories, or remote field deployments, a cloud dependency is simply not an option.

The developers who will be most valuable over the next few years are the ones who can deploy real AI — LLMs, voice models, image models — directly onto hardware. Not as a demo. As a production system that runs offline, forever, for free.

That is exactly what AI with Raspberry Pi teaches you. Starting from a $35 board, you will build the same categories of AI that the big labs are racing to miniaturize — deployed in real applications you own entirely.

Cloud AI fails on low-power hardware. Edge AI runs anywhere, anytime.
