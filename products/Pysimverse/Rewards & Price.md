## Pysimverse — Kickstarter Rewards (Draft)

This file summarizes the current reward structure and ideas discussed so far for the Pysimverse Kickstarter campaign.

---

## 1. Reward Strategy Overview

- **Core focus**: Keep the campaign centered on **Pysimverse Lifetime Access** (simulator + all future drone-domain updates).
- **Tier count**: Intentionally **minimal** for clarity and higher conversion.
- **Main revenue driver**: A single **Lifetime tier**, with a limited **Early Bird Lifetime** discount.
- **Other courses**: Treated as **optional add-ons** (or post-campaign upsells), clearly separate from Pysimverse.

---

## 2. Core Reward Tiers

### 2.1 Supporter Tier (Optional, Low-Friction)

- **Name**: Supporter / Thank You
- **Price**: \$5–\$10 (TBD)
- **Description (concept)**:
  - For backers who just want to support the project.

## 3. Add-On Strategy (Other Courses)

The following are **existing paid courses** (Tello/hardware/computer vision) that are **not part of Pysimverse**, but can be offered as **optional add-ons** on Kickstarter.

Key rules:
- Always describe them as **separate standalone courses**, not Pysimverse content.
- Pysimverse remains the **hero product** of the campaign.

### 3.1 Possible Add-On Bundles (Concepts)

These are **draft groupings**; final selection and pricing still TBD.

#### A. Drone Mastery Add-on

- **Includes**:
  - **Advanced Drone Programming** (`adv-drone-programming`)
  - **AI Drone Programming** (`ai-drone-programming`)
- **Positioning**:

> Go from simulated drones in Pysimverse to real-world AI drone flights. Learn to control a real Tello-style drone with Python, OpenCV, gestures, body tracking, and 3-axis PID control.
>  
> These are **separate standalone courses**, not part of the Pysimverse simulator. Ideal once you’re comfortable flying in the sim and want to move to real hardware.

#### B. Edge Robotics Add-on

- **Includes**:
  - **Build Your First AI Robot** (`ai-robot`)
  - **AI with Raspberry Pi** (`ai-with-rpi`)
  - **Computer Vision with Arduino** (`arduino-cv`)
  - **Arduino GUI** (`arduino-gui`)
  - **Computer Vision Jetson Nano** (`cv-jetson-nano`)
- **Positioning**:

> Build physical AI systems beyond the simulator: robots, Raspberry Pi apps, Arduino computer vision projects, and Jetson-powered robots.  
> These are **separate standalone courses** that complement Pysimverse for hands-on hardware builders.

#### C. Vision Everywhere Add-on

- **Includes**:
  - **Computer Vision with CCTV** (`cctv`)
  - **Computer Vision Game Development** (`cv-game-dev`)
  - **Computer Vision Mobile Apps** (`cv-mobile-apps`)
  - **Computer Vision Web Development** (`cv-web`)
  - **Computer Vision Interactive Games** (`interactive`)
  - **Computer Vision Smart City** (`smart-city`)
- **Positioning**:

> Take your computer vision skills beyond the simulator: AI CCTV, smart city systems, browser-based apps, mobile apps, gesture-controlled games, and interactive projections.  
> These are **separate standalone courses**, perfect once you want to deploy vision in real products and installations.

> **Note:** You can compress these into **one single “Complete Vision & Robotics Library” add-on** if fewer options are preferred.
